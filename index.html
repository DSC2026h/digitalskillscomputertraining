About Digital Skills & Computer Training

Digital Skills & Computer Training is a practical learning hub dedicated to helping students, job seekers, business owners and beginners develop valuable computer and digital skills.

Our training is designed to be beginner-friendly, practical and focused on real-world skills for employment, business and personal development.

We offer training in Computer Fundamentals, Microsoft Office, Digital Skills, Data Entry Skills, Website Building, Basic App Building, Cybersecurity, Ethical Hacking, HTML and other practical computer skills. Students can learn through physical or online classes.

Our Mission

To make practical digital and computer skills accessible and help learners gain confidence for the modern world.
